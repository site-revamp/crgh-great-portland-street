# CRGH – Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

The Centre for Reproductive and Genetic Health (CRGH) is a specialist fertility clinic based on Great Portland Street, London. Established in 1990, the clinic provides IVF, fertility investigations, genetic testing, fertility preservation and other assisted reproductive treatments.

The redesigned homepage focuses on communicating CRGH's clinical experience, specialist expertise and personalised approach to fertility care. It serves individuals and couples exploring fertility treatment, family-building options and reproductive health services.

## Main Improvements

### 1. Consistent Clinic Opening Hours and Contact Information
Addressed conflicting opening hours displayed across the website by establishing a clearer and more consistent presentation of clinic contact information. The revised approach prioritises verified opening hours and makes it easier for prospective patients to understand when they can contact the clinic or arrange appointments.

### 2. More Transparent Fertility Success-Rate Information
Improved the presentation of fertility outcome figures by giving greater visibility to reporting years, patient age groups, treatment types and measurement methods. This addresses the difficulty of comparing figures from different reporting periods and clinical categories, helping patients interpret published success rates more accurately.

### 3. Clearer Existing-Patient Communication
Improved the organisation of existing-patient contact options by separating fertility coordination, nursing enquiries and general support. The revised approach makes alternative telephone and email communication methods easier to identify, particularly following changes to the Patient Portal's messaging functionality.

## Design Improvements

- **Visual Hierarchy:** Strengthened the presentation of CRGH's clinical expertise, fertility services and primary patient enquiry opportunities.
- **Content Organisation:** Structured treatment categories, contact information and patient support details into clearer homepage sections.
- **Information Consistency:** Improved the presentation of clinic hours and contact details to reduce conflicting information.
- **Clinical Transparency:** Made fertility outcome information easier to interpret through clearer reporting context and distinctions between treatment measures.
- **Patient Experience:** Created a more accessible communication journey for prospective and existing patients.
- **Design Consistency:** Improved consistency across typography, spacing, content sections and visual elements.
- **Conversion Focus:** Made consultation enquiries, treatment information and relevant patient contact options easier to access.

## Project Objective

The redesigned homepage provides CRGH with a clearer and more trustworthy digital presence. It addresses three important areas: inconsistent contact information, fertility success-rate transparency and existing-patient communication. The overall design prioritises clinical credibility, accurate information and a more straightforward experience for individuals and couples exploring fertility care.
