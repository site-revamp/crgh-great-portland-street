The CRGH approach

Advanced medicine, guided by individual care.

Fertility care is about more than a treatment plan. It is about understanding the person, the medical picture and the choices that may be available.

CRGH brings together reproductive medicine, fertility investigations and genetic health expertise. Its approach centres on bespoke care and helping patients navigate their options with specialist support.

  • Care pathways shaped around individual circumstances.
  • Access to a broad range of fertility and reproductive services.
  • Information to help patients understand possible next steps.

Informed decisions

Understand the evidence behind treatment.

Fertility statistics can help inform decisions, but they need context. Age, treatment type, clinical history and the way a result is measured all matter.

CRGH publishes treatment success-rate information and directs patients to the HFEA for further guidance on understanding and comparing clinic data.
